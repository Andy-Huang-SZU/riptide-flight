A monumental vermilion red-orange sun partly obscured by a deep atmospheric ridge near the right third, its disk center at 67% x 43% y, radius 24% of frame height. Vast warm ivory and pale apricot sky occupying upper 60%, very slight paper fibres, sparse charcoal cloud strokes, sweeping layers of dark midnight indigo and blue-grey volcanic ridges receding in ocean mist. Nearest dark ridge sharply cut diagonally at lower left only, leave open light sky in center to composite a swallow in flight. Restrained colors: carbon indigo, Prussian blue, pale paper, persimmon. A remarkably beautiful handmade Japanese sosaku hanga x modern French adventure graphic-novel landscape print: rugged dry carved lines, opaque ink shapes, tactile broad gouache, asymmetric weight, composition using enormous simple masses. Full bleed all the way to all four edges. NO bird, NO animals, no letters, typography, borders, diagrams, any buildings or humans. One single complete painting, gallery print, striking cinematic scale.

## strata

COMPOSITION IS FLAT SIDE ELEVATION for wrapping onto 3D tunnel walls: no sky or horizon, no round hole, no existing tunnel, no vanishing point. The wall should have massive expressive hewn shapes, tapering knife marks, torn drybrush, gorgeous deep paper striations with many visible long curved gouge marks of varying thickness, not uniform wood grain or endlessly detailed noise. Sophisticated high-contrast handmade color woodblock collagraph, physical ink on cotton paper, dramatic expressive graphic art. The marks read at a thumbnail. No letters, numbers, labels, no animals, houses, human figures, ornamental frames, borders or lettering. All four edges full bleed.
