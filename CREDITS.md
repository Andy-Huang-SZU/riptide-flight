# Credits

Andy Woohoo directed *The Sun, Again / Riptide* with Codex. ImageGen produced the three paintings, twelve overlapping detail repaints, the print relief and the explanatory cutaway. The tunnel, choreography, camera, interactive reader and render pipeline were built for this project.

The projection technique was inspired by Max Vedel’s *Paris Freestyle*. [Original post](https://x.com/mxvdl/status/2120505286603505858)

## Public edition

The painted set, timing, wing-stamp process and camera are shared with the studio film. This edition includes the project’s own seven-bone procedural swallow, its editable Blender file, and a synthesised instrumental arranged on the film’s cue grid. The reference frames inside the making-of show the studio cut, which used a Tripo mesh rigged in Blender and a Suno score.

The original project code, source paintings, generated cutaway, procedural bird and procedural score in this release are provided under the [MIT license](LICENSE), to the extent the contributors hold rights in them. The film stills in the making-of illustrate the studio cut and are presentation material. The underlying Tripo mesh and Suno recording are separate studio assets.
